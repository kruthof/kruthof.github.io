This project studies how AI systems can support clinicians in complex perioperative decision-making while preserving clinical judgment, accountability, and trust.

Anesthesia involves many forms of expert knowledge work: synthesizing patient information, anticipating risks, coordinating with surgical teams, documenting decisions, communicating handoffs, and responding to uncertainty. I use this setting to study how large language models can support clinicians across these tasks without becoming opaque or overly directive.

The broader goal is to develop reliable Clinical Hybrid Intelligence: human–AI systems that help experts reason, check, revise, and communicate decisions in high-stakes professional settings.
